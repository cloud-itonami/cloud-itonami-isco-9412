# Business Model: Independent Kitchen Support Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-9412`
- ISCO-08: `9412`
- Occupation: Kitchen Helpers
- Social impact: local-jobs, food-safety, worker-safety

## Customer

- small restaurants
- catering operations
- commercial kitchens

## Offer

- dishwashing and cleaning support
- supply restocking
- food-safety checklist logging
- reporting

## Revenue

- monthly kitchen-support platform fee
- per-shift fee
- reporting package

## Trust Controls

- no robot operation near hot surfaces/open flames without governor gate
- food-safety checklist records are auditable, not editable
- sharp-tool zones require human sign-off
